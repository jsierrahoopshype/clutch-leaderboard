Methodology

A clutch event has PERIOD ≥ 4 and the absolute score margin ≤ 5. The TIME WINDOW selector further restricts which moments count: Last 5:00 (the classic NBA clutch window), Last 1:00 (final minute of regulation or any OT), or Last 0:10 (final-shot territory). Tighter windows are subsets of wider ones. PPG = clutch points / clutch games (games in which the player had at least one event in the current window).

Advanced stats

TS% is points per shooting possession with free throws counted: PTS / (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)). rTS% and PAE are both measured against the players currently qualifying rather than a fixed league average, so the bar moves as you change the window, era or minimum — PAE is the points a player scored above what an average qualifier would have managed on the same shooting possessions, which rewards volume and efficiency at once. TOV% is turnovers per possession used. LOAD% is the share of its own team's clutch possessions a player used, in the same games and window; it is not usage rate, which needs on-court time this play-by-play does not carry, and it is left blank when any of a player's seasons has no matching team row.
